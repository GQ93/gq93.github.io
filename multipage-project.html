Graph Deep Learning for Multimodal Medical Imaging Analysis

This project involved the development of innovative graph deep learning models to analyze fMRI data and predict phenotypes. The goal was to integrate multimodal data and examine relationships between different modalities in order to identify crucial biomarkers. Cutting-edge machine learning and deep learning algorithms were implemented to ensure high precision in the results. The project was carried out in collaboration with TReNDS Center (GSU/Gatech/Emory), DICoN Lab (Boys Town National Research Hospital), and Mind Research Network, and was supported by NIH and NSF grants totaling over $2 million.
